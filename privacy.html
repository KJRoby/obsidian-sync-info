Privacy Policy

Personal Obsidian Sync · Last updated: 2026/10/22

This privacy policy describes how Personal Obsidian Sync uses Google account authorization to support synchronization of an Obsidian vault with Google Drive through the GSync community plugin for Obsidian.

This is a personal project and is not an official Google or Obsidian product. It is not affiliated with or endorsed by Google, Obsidian, or the developer of the GSync plugin.

1. Purpose

The application is intended for personal use. Its purpose is to authorize the GSync plugin to access Google Drive as necessary to synchronize files belonging to the user's Obsidian vault.

2. Information and Permissions

Depending on the OAuth permissions configured for the application and the functionality used by the plugin, the synchronization process may access:

  • Google account authorization credentials, including OAuth tokens used to maintain authorized access.
  • Google Drive file metadata, such as file names, identifiers, and modification information.
  • The contents of files that the plugin reads or synchronizes, including Markdown notes, attachments, and Obsidian settings if those files are included in synchronization.

The exact access permitted is determined by the Google OAuth scopes and the plugin's implementation. Only describe permissions here that the application actually requests and uses.

3. How Information Is Used

Information accessed through Google Drive is used to provide the intended synchronization functionality between the user's Obsidian vault and Google Drive.

Google account authorization is used to authenticate requests to Google's services. Data may be transmitted between the user's device and Google when synchronization operations occur.

4. Storage and Retention

Vault files may be stored locally on the user's devices and in the user's Google Drive, according to the user's synchronization configuration.
